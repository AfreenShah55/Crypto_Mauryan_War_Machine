Chanakya's Administrative Grid (30 *Adhyakshas*)

How statecraft transformed post-war territories into a centralized bureaucratic superpower.

Samaharta (Tax Director)

Managed land revenue, urban tolls, mine royalties, and trade taxes to fund the standing imperial army.

Navadhyaksha (Naval Board)

Supervised riverine patrol fleets, coastal trade security, ferry customs, and waterborne troop transports.

Gudhapurusha Overseer

Maintained silent intelligence networks, anti-corruption monitoring of state officers, and counter-espionage.

Rajamarga Maintenance

Built the Royal Highway connecting Taxila to Pataliputra, featuring rest houses, milestone markers, and garrisons.

Primary Source Texts & Glossary

Arthashastra (Book 10, Chapter 3)

"An arrow shot by an archer may or may not kill a single person; but intrigue devised by a wise statesman can destroy even infants in the womb."

Major Rock Edict XIII (Emperor Ashoka)

"One hundred and fifty thousand were deportations, one hundred thousand were slain, and many times that number died in the Kalinga war... Now the Beloved of the Gods considers moral conquest (Dhammavijaya) to be the true conquest."

Key Sanskrit Terms
  • Vijigishu: The central monarch desiring world or subcontinental conquest.
  • Kuta-Yuddha: Deceptive or asymmetric warfare using ambushes, poison, and night raids.
  • Mlecchita Vikalpa: The ancient art of cipher writing and secret communication.
  • Chaturanga: The classic four-fold military organization (Infantry, Cavalry, Chariots, Elephants).
